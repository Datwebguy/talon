# Project

**Talon** is yield-tokenization for Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base.

xStream proved the consumer shape: one equity → income token + price token → recombine. That product was testnet xStocks with rebases. Talon is the same shape on mainnet B20, where yield is the multiplier.

## Long-term

A market per official Coinbase stock. Clip trades 24/7 as the accretion claim. Talon is the principal claim and, later, the session price market against USDC using Chainlink total-return feeds. Fees on tear/join can accrue to clip holders (xStream: 80/20). Auction/lease of clip is phase 2.

## Quest slice (still real)

Mainnet vault on AAPLc, live reads, public app, Loom of a real tear/join. Other official tickers are factory params, not mocks.
