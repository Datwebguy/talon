# Talon launch post draft

Base builders are making US market exposure usable onchain.

We built Talon for eligible non US users who want to use official Coinbase Tokenized Stocks on Base without starting with a maze of DEX terminology.

Talon starts with official AAPLc on Base mainnet. Deposit it into the Talon vault and receive two equal raw token claims:

Clip for the B20 multiplier leg.

Talon for the price leg.

Keep the exposure you want, transfer a claim to another wallet, or recombine equal Clip and Talon to get the underlying AAPLc amount back.

The product is non custodial. Coinbase issues the stock token. Base is the network. Contract addresses stay visible so every user can verify what they are using.

We are building toward a simpler way to use tokenized stocks on Base: less jargon, clearer ownership, and programmable exposure for eligible non US users.

Try it: https://talononbase.tech

Built for the @buildonbase Builder Quest.

Stock actions are not enabled for US persons. This is not an offer or recommendation to buy or sell securities.
